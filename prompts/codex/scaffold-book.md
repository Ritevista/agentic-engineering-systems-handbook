# Scaffold Book Prompt (Short Form)

Create an mdBook scaffold for **Agentic Engineering Field Manual** using concise, reader-facing chapter stubs.

Requirements:
- Keep content concise and reference-oriented.
- Use the Nexus Engineering Control Plane case study consistently.
- Avoid writing full chapters.
- Do not expose internal planning headings such as `Purpose`, `Key Questions`, `Planned Sections`, `Nexus Case Study Connection`, or `To be expanded` in public chapters.
- Each public chapter stub should include a reader problem, design principle, Nexus case study, and quick reference.
- Keep terminology consistent across agent, subagent, steering, skill, slash command, hook, permissions, context, specs/plans/tasks, artifacts, verification, and MCP/tooling.
